Set your two alarms

This app can't buzz you, so let your phone's clock do the nudging.

  1. Open the Clock app on your phone and tap Alarm.
  2. Add an alarm for coffee time. Set it to repeat every day and name it "Dragon: walk and morning treat".
  3. Add a second alarm for the evening, repeating every day, named "Dragon: evening treat".
The three rules
  1. A missed day costs nothing. There is no starting over, just the next day.
  2. Try not to miss two days in a row.
  3. No adding more for the first two weeks, even on good days.

Everything here is saved on this phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
